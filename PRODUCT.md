# TutorMatch

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js on Vercel, Firebase Authentication/Firestore, Cloudinary media, payOS payments.

## Users
Guests searching for a tutor; students buying lessons; tutors offering one-to-one lessons; administrators resolving money and session issues.

## Product Purpose
Find online or in-person tutors and manage prepaid lesson packages, session confirmations, refunds, and tutor withdrawals.

## Positioning
Package money is allocated to tutor earnings only as individual lessons are confirmed by both parties.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Google sign-in. Tutors create sessions after agreeing on a time with students. Both confirm within 24 hours after the scheduled end. Missing or conflicting confirmations go to an administrator. payOS handles incoming payments; administrators manually transfer refunds and withdrawals. No homework feature.

## Brand Commitments
Vietnamese-first, bright Gen Z style, cute little star mascot. Accessible on both phone and desktop.

## Evidence on Hand
No tutor profiles or photos have been supplied. Do not invent real tutors, ratings, or testimonials.
